As part of my work on CUSail’s sails and rigging system, I designed several custom components to improve the functionality and reliability of the sail-control system, including servo mounts, servo wheel heads, and a mast-boom connector.

I designed custom servo mounts in CAD around the dimensions of our selected servos and the limited space available inside the hull. The mounts needed to securely position the servos while maintaining proper alignment with the rigging system.

">

During testing, we encountered an issue where the rigging line could wrap around the neck of the servo wheel and jam the mechanism. 

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/Old1.png' | relative_url }}" width="300
">
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/Old2.png' | relative_url }}" width="300
">

To solve this, I redesigned the wheel head with a more enclosed geometry and controlled line-entry point. I also incorporated pulleys to improve the angle at which the line approached the servo. The redesigned system was 3D printed, tested, and successfully eliminated the tangling issue during sailing.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/NewSH.png' | relative_url }}" width="500
">

I also worked on the mast-boom connector, which joins the horizontal boom to the vertical mast while still allowing the boom to rotate as the sail changes position. The connector had to securely support the boom without unnecessarily restricting its motion. I designed the component around the geometry of the existing mast and boom, considering fit, range of motion, loading, and ease of assembly. Like the servo components, the ability to rapidly prototype the connector allowed the design to be adjusted based on fit and functionality.

<img src="{{ '/assets/images/MB.png' | relative_url }}" width="500
">

These projects gave me experience using CAD, 3D printing, prototyping, and iterative mechanical design to create custom components for a larger working system. Rather than designing parts independently, I had to consider how each component interacted with the rigging, sails, servos, mast, and boom to improve the overall reliability of the boat.
