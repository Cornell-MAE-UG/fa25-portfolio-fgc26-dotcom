As part of my CAD design work, I designed a functional coffee grinder assembly in Autodesk Fusion, focusing on both mechanical operation and realistic component integration. I modeled the primary components of the grinder and imported burr gears from McMaster-Carr, gaining experience incorporating commercially available components into a custom design.

I used Autodesk Fusion’s joint functions to define the motion and interaction between components. Revolute and rigid joints connected the handle, shaft, and grinding mechanism so that the assembly could demonstrate its intended function. When the handle was rotated in the CAD model, the connected burr gear rotated with it, simulating the grinding motion that would crush coffee beans between the burrs. This allowed me to visualize the mechanism in motion and verify that the components interacted correctly.

This project strengthened my skills in 3D modeling, mechanical assemblies, joint constraints, motion simulation, and integrating real-world components into CAD designs.
